CoachUrself

A workout tracker for people who follow their own training: build workouts and programs, run sessions with a rest timer, and track records, volume and body measurements over time. iOS, Android and web. I co-founded it with a backend developer, and it's in closed alpha.

  • The whole Flutter appFrom requirements to closed alpha: every screen below, on Clean Architecture and Riverpod.
  • Offline-first dataEvery change is saved locally first (Drift/SQLite) and synced in order. Items created offline get temporary IDs that are swapped for the server's once they sync, so the full training loop works in airplane mode.
  • Native Android live workoutA Kotlin foreground service, driven over a MethodChannel, keeps the session and rest timer in the notification, so sets can be logged or rest skipped without opening the app.
  • Nearly 900 automated testsCI runs them in four time zones, from UTC−8 to UTC+12:45, to catch date and daylight-saving bugs, then signs and ships staging or production builds to testers.
  • WebAbout half of the React and TypeScript web app.

Ashborne: Survival Roguelite

A survival roguelite played in portrait with one thumb. Pick one of six heroes, each with a signature spell that grows from rank 1 to 12, and hold out against a horde that grows with the clock, with a boss every minute. Reaching ten minutes is a feat. Plays offline; every ad is an optional video.

  • The whole game, aloneIn Unity 6 (URP, mobile): six heroes with their own spells and cards, ten bosses, four maps, timed events and challenges, meta progression and a bestiary.
  • A horde of hundredsEnemies moved by C# Jobs compiled with Burst and drawn in instanced batches.
  • Hand-written shadersSpell effects in HLSL: fireballs, shadow bolts, arrows with trails, a poison cloud, the Ranger's camouflage.
  • Monetization without pressureRewarded videos only (LevelPlay mediation) and a one-time Supporter Pack plus tips through Google Play Billing.
  • About 4,000 automated testsPlus a headless bot that plays whole runs to measure balance between heroes.
