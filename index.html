Co-founder · Flutter · Closed alpha CoachUrself A fitness app for iOS, Android and web that I co-founded with a backend developer, now in closed alpha. What I built:
  • The entire Flutter app, from requirements to closed alpha, on Clean Architecture and Riverpod.
  • Offline-first sync on Drift/SQLite: what users log without a connection waits in a local queue and is sent in order when they're back online.
  • A native Android notification for the live workout (a Kotlin foreground service driven over a MethodChannel): users complete sets, add rest time or skip it without opening the app.
  • 1,000+ automated tests that CI runs before every build, with separate staging and production releases.
